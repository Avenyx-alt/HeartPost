# HeartPost demo video script (2:00, three voices)

A screen recording with voice-over; no editing needed beyond joining the parts. Don't say names or schools: Round 1 is judged blind.
About 270 words in total. Speak calmly, and keep the mouse slow so judges can follow.

---

## Part 1 · Speaker A (captain) · 0:00–0:15 · The problem
**Screen:** HeartPost home page, top of the page.

> Almost half of US counties have no cardiologist. In rural areas, the nearest one is an 87-mile round trip. So a nurse at a small clinic has to decide: which patient with chest pain really needs that trip? That's what HeartPost helps with.

## Part 2 · Speaker A (captain) · 0:15–0:45 · Demo, first patient
**Screen:** click **"Man, 46 · Long Beach VA"** → red zone appears. Open **"How this score was calculated"**, then point at the angiography box.

> HeartPost uses only seven things a small clinic can measure, like age, type of chest pain and blood pressure. This is a real patient our model never saw. Red zone: refer soon. And it isn't a black box. Every score is a short sum of points you can check by hand. His real angiography result: heart disease. The model got it right.

## Part 3 · Speaker B · 0:45–1:15 · Demo, fairness and failure
**Screen:** click **"Woman, 55 · Cleveland"** → yellow, point at the box under the result. Then click **"Woman, 47 · Hungary"** → green, point at the red-bordered box.

> This woman was sick too. With one threshold for everyone, she'd have been sent home. We found the model was missing half of sick women, so women get their own threshold, and now she's caught. But we don't hide mistakes. This woman also had heart disease, and the model said green. Young patients with unusual pain are its weak spot, and the app warns about it.

## Part 4 · Speaker C · 1:15–1:45 · How we tested it
**Screen:** scroll to **"Every patient the model never saw"**. Drag the threshold right, then back. Untick **"Separate threshold for women"**, then tick it again.

> We trained on one US hospital and tested on three others the model never saw. Here are all 678 test patients. Move the threshold: you catch more sick people, but get more false alarms. A textbook model with thirteen tests missed 93 sick patients in the new hospitals. Ours missed 43. Now switch off the women's threshold, and ten sick women turn red: missed.

## Part 5 · Speaker A (captain) · 1:45–2:00 · Limitation and next step
**Screen:** click **"Remote follow-up (demo)"** in the header and show the clinician dashboard.

> Our data is from the 1980s, so next we'd test on today's patients with real clinicians, and grow this home follow-up demo. HeartPost: seven simple measurements, fair to women, and honest about its mistakes.

### Recording checklist
- Record in a browser window at full screen. Close other tabs and notifications.
- Reload the page before recording so the result panel is empty.
- Each speaker records their own part; join the parts in order (any free editor, or QuickTime "Add clip to end").
- Total must be 2:00 or less. If you run long, cut words, not the clicks on screen.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** and check the link in a private window.
